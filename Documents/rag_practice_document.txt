1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
============================================================

BrightPath Technologies is a fictional software company with offices in Hyderabad,
Bengaluru, and Pune. The company develops web applications, data platforms, and
AI-powered business tools.

The Hyderabad office is located at 4th Floor, Orion Business Center, Madhapur,
Hyderabad, Telangana 500081. The office reception is open Monday through Friday
from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

The company's standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through
Friday. Teams may have different schedules if their manager approves them.

============================================================
2. LEAVE POLICY
============================================================

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid leave and 12 days of sick leave
per calendar year. Leave balances are prorated for employees who join during
the year.

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the People Portal at
least three working days before the leave begins. Their manager must approve
the request before the leave is considered approved.

For unexpected illness, employees should inform their manager as soon as
possible. A medical certificate may be requested for sick leave lasting more
than three consecutive working days.

Unused paid leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of five days into
the next calendar year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or converted to
cash.

Contractors follow the leave terms in their individual contracts and do not
automatically receive the employee leave allowance described above.

============================================================
3. REMOTE WORK POLICY
============================================================

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Remote days should be recorded in the People Portal by the previous Friday.

Employees must use the company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems
from outside the office. Confidential company data must not be downloaded to
personal devices.

Employees who handle restricted customer information must work from an
approved private location and use a company-managed device.

Remote work is not automatically available to contractors; their arrangement
must be confirmed in their contract or with the project manager.

============================================================
4. IT SUPPORT AND ACCESS
============================================================

Employees can create an IT ticket through the HelpDesk Portal. The support
team operates Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

For a standard laptop or software issue, the target first response time is
four business hours. For a critical outage affecting an entire team, the
target first response time is 30 minutes during support hours.

To request access to a new application, employees must submit an Access
Request ticket, identify the application, explain the business reason, and
name their manager. The manager and application owner must approve access.

Passwords must never be shared through email or chat. If an employee suspects
that their account has been compromised, they should contact IT immediately
and use the password reset page from a trusted device.

For urgent security incidents outside support hours, call the on-call security
number listed in the company directory. Do not invent or guess this number.

============================================================
5. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
============================================================

Business travel expenses must be submitted through the Expense Portal within
30 calendar days after the trip ends.

Receipts are required for individual expenses of INR 500 or more. Claims must
include the date, business purpose, expense category, and project code.

Manager approval is required before Finance processes a reimbursement.
Approved claims are normally paid within ten business days. Missing receipts
or incorrect project codes may delay payment.

Personal purchases, traffic fines, and expenses without a valid business
purpose are not reimbursable.

============================================================
6. INFORMATION SECURITY
============================================================

Employees must lock their computers whenever they leave their workstations.
Company data must be stored only in approved company systems.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, source control, and
cloud administration accounts.

Employees must not paste confidential source code, customer records, API keys,
passwords, or access tokens into public AI tools.

Suspected phishing emails should be reported using the Report Phishing option
in the email client. Employees should not open suspicious attachments or enter
their credentials on links from unexpected messages.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT immediately.

============================================================
7. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
============================================================

Each full-time employee may claim up to INR 20,000 per calendar year for
approved job-related courses or certifications.

Before purchasing a course, the employee must submit a Learning Request in the
People Portal. The manager and Learning and Development team must approve it.

Reimbursement requires proof of payment and a completion certificate, where
the course provides one. Unapproved courses are not eligible for reimbursement.

The allowance cannot be transferred between employees or carried forward to
the next year.

============================================================
8. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
============================================================

Formal performance reviews take place twice each year, in April and October.

Employees should submit a self-review before the deadline announced by HR.
Managers evaluate delivery, technical skills, collaboration, communication,
and progress against agreed goals.

A performance rating does not automatically guarantee a salary increase or
promotion. Compensation decisions depend on performance, business needs,
budget, and internal compensation guidelines.

Employees who disagree with review feedback should first discuss it with their
manager. If the concern remains unresolved, they may contact HR.

============================================================
9. ONBOARDING CHECKLIST
============================================================

New employees should complete the following steps during their first week:

1. Activate their company email and MFA.
2. Review the Information Security Policy.
3. Confirm payroll and emergency contact details in the People Portal.
4. Complete required compliance training.
5. Request access to project repositories and development environments.
6. Meet their manager to review team goals and onboarding expectations.

IT equipment requests should be submitted at least five business days before
the employee's start date when possible. Equipment delivery depends on stock
and location.

   date.

   cannot be carried forward.

Q: When should I submit business travel expenses?

Q: What is the annual learning allowance?

Q: Can I upload customer data to a public AI chatbot to debug an issue?

Q: What should I do if I suspect my account has been compromised?
A: Contact IT immediately and use the password reset page from a trusted device.

Q: What is the exact on-call security phone number?

   Do not guess the number.
